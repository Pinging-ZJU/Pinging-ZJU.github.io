I am a ZJU 100-Talent Program Professor at the State Key Laboratory of Blockchain and Data Security, Zhejiang University. I received my Ph.D. degree from the College of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang University, where I was advised by Prof. Shuibing He and Prof. Xian-He Sun.

My research focuses on intelligent computing systems and AI infrastructure, with an emphasis on efficient large-scale model training and inference. My research interests include large model memory management, AI-native storage systems, training acceleration, LLM inference optimization, heterogeneous computing, and AI cluster scheduling.

I have led or participated in multiple research projects. I serve as Principal Investigator of a project supported by the Young Scientists Fund of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the CCF-Baidu Pinecone Fund, as Task Leader in the National Key R&D Program of China (Young Scientist Project), and as Co-Principal Investigator of an Ant Group research project. I have also participated in the Zhejiang Province "Pioneer" Program, State Grid research projects, the National Natural Science Foundation of China General Program, Zhejiang Provincial Major Science and Technology Projects, and the Zhejiang Provincial New Seedling Talent Program.

My research has been published in leading computer systems journals and conferences, including FAST, EuroSys, SIGMOD, KDD, IEEE TC, IEEE TPDS, ICPP, CLUSTER and other top-tier systems conferences. I aim to build efficient and scalable AI systems for next-generation intelligent computing platforms.

陈平，男，博士，杭州高新区（滨江）区块链与数据安全研究院/ 浙江大学区块链与数据安全全国重点实验室研究员， CCF信息存储技术专业委员会执行委员、CCF体系结构专业委员会执行委员， 于浙江大学计算机科学与技术学院取得博士学位， 曾获浙江大学国家奖学金、浙江大学优秀毕业生等荣誉。

长期从事大模型训练与推理系统优化、高性能存储系统等方向研究， 主要聚焦于大模型训练与推理过程中的存储优化、显存管理与数据访问加速等关键技术。 近年来，与百度、华为云、之江实验室等国内头部企业及科研机构开展合作研究， 负责智能计算集群中大模型训练与推理模块的研发与性能调优， 目前在蚂蚁集团担任访问学者，参与大模型推理KV存储系统的加速。 围绕大模型训练与推理场景下高效数据访问与存储需求， 提出了一系列面向训练缓存、显存扩展与推理加速的存储优化方法， 实现了存储空间与带宽资源的高效利用， 显著降低系统运行成本并提升整体训练与推理效率。 相关成果已在华为云昇腾产品线、之江实验室“万卡工程”智能计算集群 及阿里云相关存储项目中完成工程化落地并实现实际应用，减少30%成本。

主持或参与科研项目多项， 主持国家自然科学基金青年科学基金项目（C类）、CCF–百度松果基金， 担任国家重点研发计划青年科学家项目课题负责人， 联合主持蚂蚁集团科研合作项目， 并参与浙江省“尖兵”计划、国家电网科研合作、 国家自然科学基金面上项目、浙江省重大专项及浙江省新苗人才计划等项目。

Research Interests

My research explores system-level optimization techniques for large-scale AI workloads. I study efficient AI infrastructure from the perspectives of memory, storage, computation, and scheduling. Current research topics include:

  • Large model training systems and memory optimization
  • LLM inference systems and KV cache management
  • AI data pipeline acceleration and checkpoint optimization
  • AI-native storage systems and heterogeneous memory computing
  • Large-scale AI cluster scheduling and resource management

研究方向

主要研究面向大规模人工智能负载的系统级优化技术， 从内存、存储、计算与调度等角度研究高效智能计算基础设施。 目前主要研究方向包括：

  • 大模型训练系统与显存优化
  • 大语言模型推理系统与 KV Cache 管理
  • AI 数据流水线加速与检查点优化
  • AI 原生存储系统与异构内存计算
  • 大规模智能计算集群调度与资源管理

Research Team

I work with a research team focusing on efficient AI systems and intelligent computing infrastructure. The team investigates large model training, inference optimization, memory management, storage systems, and heterogeneous computing platforms. We collaborate with universities and industry partners to develop practical AI system solutions.

研究团队

团队主要围绕高效人工智能系统与智能计算基础设施开展研究， 重点研究大模型训练与推理优化、显存管理、高性能存储系统 以及异构计算平台等方向。 团队与国内高校、科研机构及产业界保持长期合作， 面向大规模智能计算场景开展系统关键技术研究与工程化应用。

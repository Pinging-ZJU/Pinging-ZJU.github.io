陈平 (Ping Chen)

ZJU 100-Talent Program Professor [百人计划研究员]
State Key Laboratory of Blockchain and Data Security, Zhejiang University

I am a ZJU 100-Talent Program Professor at the State Key Laboratory of Blockchain and Data Security, Zhejiang University. I received my Ph.D. degree from the College of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang University, where I was advised by Prof. Shuibing He and Prof. Xian-He Sun.

My research focuses on intelligent computing systems and AI infrastructure, with an emphasis on efficient large-scale model training and inference. My research interests include large model memory management, AI-native storage systems, training acceleration, LLM inference optimization, heterogeneous computing, and AI cluster scheduling.

My research has been published in leading computer systems journals and conferences, including IEEE TC, IEEE TPDS, ACM TOS, USENIX FAST, and other top-tier systems conferences. I aim to build efficient and scalable AI systems for next-generation intelligent computing platforms.

Research Interests

My research explores system-level optimization techniques for large-scale AI workloads. I study efficient AI infrastructure from the perspectives of memory, storage, computation, and scheduling. Current research topics include:

  • Large model training systems and memory optimization
  • LLM inference systems and KV cache management
  • AI data pipeline acceleration and checkpoint optimization
  • AI-native storage systems and heterogeneous memory computing
  • Large-scale AI cluster scheduling and resource management

Research Team

I work with a research team focusing on efficient AI systems and intelligent computing infrastructure. The team investigates large model training, inference optimization, memory management, storage systems, and heterogeneous computing platforms. We collaborate with universities and industry partners to develop practical AI system solutions.
